Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Madrasa ("we", "the app") is a school management and parent-communication service. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your phone number — to sign you in (via a one-time verification code) and identify your role (director, teacher, parent).
  • Your name, if you or your school provide it.
  • School data entered by schools: classes, subjects, timetables, students' names, grades, attendance and announcements — entered and controlled by each school's staff.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: setting up a school, recording attendance and grades, letting parents view their child's grades and attendance, and sending relevant notifications.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We use a few service providers strictly to run the app:

  • Supabase — secure database and authentication.
  • ChinqIT — delivery of the one-time sign-in code by SMS/WhatsApp.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — push notifications.

Access is restricted by role: a parent sees only their own child; a teacher only their own classes; a director only their own school.

Data retention & account deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (account menu → Delete account), which permanently removes your login and personal profile.

Children's data

Students' information is entered by their school, which is responsible for having the appropriate authorization from parents/guardians. Parents access only their own child's information.

Security

Data is protected in transit and at rest, and access is enforced by row-level security rules on the server.

Politique de confidentialité (FR)

Madrasa est un service de gestion scolaire et de communication avec les parents. Nous collectons votre numéro de téléphone (connexion par code à usage unique et identification de votre rôle), votre nom le cas échéant, et les données scolaires saisies par les écoles (classes, matières, emplois du temps, élèves, notes, présences, annonces).

Nous ne vendons pas vos données et ne vous suivons pas dans d'autres applications. Nous utilisons Supabase (base de données), ChinqIT (envoi du code par SMS/WhatsApp) et Firebase (notifications) uniquement pour faire fonctionner l'application. L'accès est limité par rôle.

Vous pouvez supprimer votre compte à tout moment depuis l'application (menu Compte → Supprimer le compte). Les données des élèves sont saisies par l'école, responsable des autorisations nécessaires.
