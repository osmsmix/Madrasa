# Privacy Policy — Madrasa

_Last updated: 2026-09-24_

Madrasa ("we", "the app") is a school management and parent-communication service.
This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. For any question, contact
us at **oussama.mohamedelhadi@gmail.com**.

## Information we collect
- **Your phone number**, used to sign you in (via a one-time verification code) and
  to identify your role (director, teacher, or parent).
- **Your name**, if you or your school provide it.
- **School data entered by schools**: classes, subjects, timetables, students'
  names, grades, attendance and announcements. This information is entered and
  controlled by each school's staff.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

## How we use it
Only to provide the service: setting up a school, recording attendance and grades,
letting parents view their child's grades and attendance, and sending relevant
notifications.

## Sharing
We do **not** sell your data. We use a small number of service providers strictly
to run the app:
- **Supabase** — secure database and authentication (data hosting).
- **ChinqIT** — delivery of the one-time sign-in code by SMS/WhatsApp.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)** — delivery of push notifications.

Access to data inside the app is restricted by role: a parent sees only their own
child; a teacher sees only their own classes; a director sees only their own school.

## Data retention & account deletion
Your data is kept while your account is active. You can **delete your account at any
time from within the app** (account menu → Delete account), which permanently
removes your login and personal profile. Schools remain responsible for the school
records they enter.

## Children's data
Students' information is entered by their school, which is responsible for having the
appropriate authorization from parents/guardians. Parents access only their own
child's information.

## Security
Data is protected in transit and at rest, and access is enforced by row-level
security rules on the server.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
